# Claim verification

1. List every factual statement in the changed copy: numbers, dates, client names, roles,
   response times, scope limits, outcomes, comparisons, "best/most/only" language.
2. For each, find the evidence: a public URL with access date, a file in this repo, or written
   client permission. Record it in `research/claim-register.md` (status V / M / R).
3. Block (remove or rewrite) anything that:
   - states traffic, ranking, inquiry or revenue results without client-supplied data and permission;
   - guarantees rankings, leads or revenue, or implies paid placement;
   - quotes a person without full name, title and permission;
   - implies scarcity without a capacity record;
   - calls Mansoor an engineer or suggests engineering services (California BPELSG title rules);
   - uses "unlimited", "premium", "world-class", "revolutionize", "seamless", "elevate", "unlock".
4. Mark business commitments (response times, scope, terms) as **M** until Mansoor confirms them.
5. Report: claims checked, removed, and the open **M** items.
