# Personalized outreach draft

Inputs: one prospect row (with source URLs) and the family to use (verified gap, growth trigger,
peer proof). Templates and the required footer are in `research/message-library.md`.

1. Re-open the source URLs and confirm each observation is still true today. If not, stop.
2. If the row has fewer than one strong, specific, verifiable observation, do not draft.
3. Write 80–140 words: why this firm; one or two observations; the consequence as a possibility;
   one relevant proof point (Oaktown for structural, Bear for geotech, otherwise both); one
   low-friction ask (three-point screen recording or a 20-minute call).
4. Add the footer: identity, postal address, solicitation disclosure, opt-out.
5. Cite the evidence used under the draft (row id and columns).
6. Forbidden: fake "Re:", fake referrals, urgency, guilt, tracking pixels, personal emails,
   pitching through careers/permit/emergency/accessibility channels.
7. Output to Mansoor for approval. Never send, schedule or submit anything.
