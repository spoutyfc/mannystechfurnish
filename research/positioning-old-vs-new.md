# Positioning: old versus new

| | Old (live site, July 2026 build) | New (branch `aec/growth-partner`) |
|---|---|---|
| Category | "Websites that actually convert" for any business | Website and local search partner for engineering and construction firms |
| Audience | Unstated; small businesses generally | Principals of Bay Area structural, geotechnical, civil, surveying, MEP, GEC and design-build firms |
| Offer | $1,899 one-time; $900 + $159/mo (3-month min); $99/mo care; six add-ons $179–$349 and $49/mo | One offer: $1,000/month, six-month term, build included |
| How to buy | Live Stripe "Pay & get started" buttons before any scope | Fit call, written scope, signed agreement, first payment at kickoff |
| Proof | Unverified metrics, initials-only testimonials | Live client sites with dated, checkable page counts; no results claims |
| Voice | "I" and "we" mixed; "Web Studio — Est. 2023" | First person singular throughout; one named developer |
| Visual | Black/magenta, WebGL orb, videos, preloader, smooth scroll | Light, ruled drawing-sheet layout; no decorative motion |
| CTA | "Start a project" / pay links | "Book a 20-minute fit call" |

## Homepage hero

**Chosen:** "Websites and local search for engineering and construction firms." — names the
category and the buyer in the first line; a principal knows in five seconds whether to keep reading.

**Alternate (not built):** "Your project record is your best salesperson. Most firm websites hide it."
Sharper and more emotional, but it needs the second line to say who it is for, and it leans on a
claim about "most firm websites" that is an observation, not measured. Worth an A/B test only after
there is enough traffic to read a result.
